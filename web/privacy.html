RepoSwipe Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Applies to the RepoSwipe iOS app, the web app at swipe.yeete.ai and the RepoSwipe browser extension

RepoSwipe is built to be private by design. There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in RepoSwipe — the repos you swipe, your Stack, achievements, pack progress, channel settings and language — is stored only on your device (in the app's local storage). We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it. You can export it yourself at any time from the Stack screen and restore it from that backup.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

  • GitHub's public API (api.github.com) — to fetch public repository data (names, descriptions, stars, READMEs). These requests carry no information about you beyond what any web request includes (your IP address, handled under GitHub's privacy statement).
  • Optional GitHub token. If you choose to paste a personal access token to raise GitHub's rate limit, it is stored only on your device and sent only to api.github.com. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deliberately excluded from backups you export.
  • Optional AI keys and MCP servers. If you choose to connect your own Claude, ChatGPT or Perplexity API key, or add an MCP server, those keys and server addresses are stored only on your device and sent only to the provider or server you entered (api.anthropic.com, api.openai.com, api.perplexity.ai, or your MCP server's URL). Your questions and the repository details you ask about go to that provider under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent to us, and keys are excluded from backups you export.
  • Optional share links. If you tap "Share link", the list of public repositories in your Stack is uploaded to our share service so the link can display it. The link contains repository names and metadata only — nothing that identifies you. A random, anonymous device identifier is used so you can update your own link; it isn't tied to any personal information.
  • Opening links. Tapping "Open on GitHub", "Fork", or any of the "Send to AI" options opens that third-party site (GitHub, Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity or Grok) in a browser sheet. From that point, their privacy policies apply. The repository link is included in what you open; nothing else about you is passed along.
  • Browser extension. The RepoSwipe extension adds a button to github.com repository pages. It reads only the repository name from the page you are on, and only when you click the button, to open that repo in RepoSwipe. It collects nothing, stores nothing, and makes no network requests of its own.

What we never do

  • No analytics, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers.
  • No collection of your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is none to sell.

Children

RepoSwipe is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
